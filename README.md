# NBA Player Value Analysis - 2023-24 Season

## Project Overview

This analysis evaluates all NBA players from the 2023-24 season to identify who were the most valuable to their teams using comprehensive data from the NBA API. The goal is to find players who provide significant value but may not receive enough recognition for their contributions.

## Objective

Identify underrated players who were valuable to their teams by examining efficiency metrics, overall impact, and defensive contributions beyond traditional scoring statistics.

## Key Metrics Used

- **PIE (Player Impact Estimate)**: Overall contribution across all statistical categories
- **Net Rating**: Team's point differential when player is on court
- **True Shooting Percentage**: Shooting efficiency including free throws
- **Usage Rate**: Percentage of team plays used by the player
- **Defensive Rating**: Points allowed per 100 possessions when player is on court
- **Assist/Rebound Percentage**: Player's share of team assists/rebounds

## Data Sources

- NBA API for comprehensive player statistics
- 2023-24 season data including advanced metrics
- Minimum 15-20 games played for statistical significance

## Key Findings

The analysis identifies players who provide significant value through:
- High efficiency scoring
- Strong defensive contributions
- Playmaking and ball movement
- Overall team impact beyond traditional box score statistics

This helps identify underrated players who deserve more recognition for their contributions to team success.
